# LinkedIn Services — Proposal Playbook (Thanks UX)

> Goal: LinkedIn theke AI workflow, UI/UX design, marketing ar website building service er client ana —
> profile improve kora theke shuru kore proposal pathano, follow-up, deal close porjonto puro system.
>
> Bhasha: explanation Banglish e, kintu client ke pathano sob message **English** e (global client).

---

## 0. Screenshot e ja holo — eta kivabe kaj kore

Joy ekta **project request** post korechilo (Resume Writing). LinkedIn oi request ta matching
service provider der dekhay. Nageswara er **Service Page** e "Resume Writing" chilo, tai se request ta
peyeche ar **proposal** pathiyeche.

Mane: **LinkedIn e cold proposal pathano jay na.** Proposal jay shudhu tokhon, jokhon keu
project request post kore ar tomar Service Page er category match kore. Tai kaj duita:

1. **Inbound** — Service Page ready rakho, jate request asle tumi proposal dite paro (ei playbook er main part).
2. **Outbound** — nijer network e DM / content diye client toiri koro (section 6).

### Nageswara er message keno kaj korlo (copy korar moto pattern)

| Step | Ki korlo | Keno kaj kore |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Personal opener | "With your focus on designing engaging user interfaces…" | Client er profile porche — generic mone hoy na |
| 2. Outcome-first bullets | Headline, keywords, projects, recruiter-ready | Client ki pabe sheta clear, "ami ki kori" na |
| 3. Differentiator | "Unlimited revisions" | Onnano proposal theke alada kore |
| 4. Bonus | Cover letter + LinkedIn optimization | Value beshi mone hoy, dam na barie |
| 5. Soft CTA | "Let me know if you're interested" | Pressure nai |
| 6. Chat e short intro | Proposal card er upore 3 line | Inbox e dekhte clean |
| 7. Price age bole nai | "Share your resume so I can review and share pricing" | Asset chaye engagement barano + custom quote |
| 8. Follow-up | ~10 ghonta pore 1 ta polite follow-up | Reply rate onek bare |

**Weak point:** proposal e kono portfolio/proof, timeline, ba price range nai. Amra eta better korbo.

## 5. Phase 5 — Follow-up, pricing, close

### Timing
| Kokhon | Ki pathabo |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Proposal + short intro |
| Client reply korle | 1 ghontar moddhe reply; 1 ta **asset** chao (current site / app / brief) — Nageswara er "share your resume" er moto |
| Reply na korle, +1 day | Follow-up #1 (niche) |
| +4 days | Follow-up #2 — value add (1 ta quick tip / mini audit screenshot) |
| +10 days | Last — "closing the loop" message, tarpor stop |

### Follow-up #1
```
Hi {Name}, hope you're doing well! Just following up on my proposal for your
{project}. If you share {current site / brief}, I can send a tailored plan and
price within 24 hours. Happy to answer any questions.
```

### Follow-up #2 (value add)
```
Hi {Name}, I took a quick look at {their site/app} and noticed 3 quick wins:
1) … 2) … 3) …
Happy to walk you through these on a 15-min call — no obligation.
```

### Client "details discuss" bolle (Joy er moto)
1. Thank + 3–4 ta specific question (goal, deadline, budget range, reference).
2. 15–20 min call offer (Google Meet / Calendly link).
3. Call er por 24 ghontar moddhe: scope + timeline + price + payment terms (50% advance).
4. Payment: Wise / Payoneer / bank; small project e 100% advance.

### Pricing packages (starting point — nijer market onujayi adjust koro)
| Service | Starter | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| UI/UX | 1 flow / 5 screens | 15 screens + prototype | Full product + design system |
| Website | 1-page landing | 5 pages + CMS | Custom / e-commerce |
| AI workflow | 1 automation | 3 connected workflows | Custom AI agent + integrations |
| Marketing | 12 posts/month | Posts + ads creative | Full funnel + landing page |

## 8. Do / Don't

**Do**: fast reply, personalize, proof link, clear next step, 1 ta asset chao, polite follow-up.
**Don't**: copy-paste generic message, 4 ta service eksathe ek proposal e pitch (request er sathe jeta match kore shudhu sheta),
lomba paragraph, price diye shuru, 3 bar er beshi follow-up.
