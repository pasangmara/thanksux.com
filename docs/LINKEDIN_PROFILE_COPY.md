## About

Hi, I'm Joy, a UI/UX and graphic designer from Dhaka, Bangladesh, and the founder of Thanks UX.

For 4+ years I've designed for online marketplaces and agencies: SaaS dashboards, responsive websites, brand identities and marketing materials. I work end-to-end, from understanding the problem and requirements, through wireframes, UI design and prototypes, to a clean handoff your developers can build from.

What I can help you with:

🎨 UI/UX Design: SaaS dashboards, web apps and mobile screens that are easy to use and consistent, built on a reusable design system in Figma.
🌐 Website Design & Build: fast, responsive websites and landing pages that explain your offer clearly and turn visitors into enquiries.
⚡ AI Workflow Automation: n8n workflows that take repetitive work off your plate, like lead capture, follow-up emails, reports and AI-assisted replies.
📣 Branding & Marketing Design: logos, brand systems, social media creatives and print materials that make your business look as good as it is.

How I work:
• I start with your goal and your users, not with the visuals
• You see progress early (wireframes before polish), so there are no surprises
• Clear timelines, regular updates, and revisions until it's right
• Proper handover: organized Figma files, specs and assets

I mostly work with startup founders, small business owners and agencies who need one person to handle both the product and the brand side.

Tools: Figma, Adobe XD, Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, n8n
