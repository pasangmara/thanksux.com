> **[Problem]** By morning, half of them have bought somewhere else. A cash-on-delivery parcel comes back. The boost brought likes, not sales. And your logo, your website and your chatbot don't talk to each other.

> **[Promise]** What if one team fixed all of it — brand, website, AI replies and ads, built as one system? This is ARGUS. By the end, you'll know exactly which part your business needs.

>
> **[CREATE · Website]** Then a home you own: a landing page from fifteen thousand, or a full online store with bKash, Nagad, cards and cash on delivery.
>
> **[CREATE · Content]** And a page that looks alive every week: designed posts, stories and reels — from fifteen thousand a month.
>
> **[AUTOMATE · AI reply]** Step three: automate. Remember that one a.m. message? Now it's answered in seconds — in Bangla, Banglish or English — with your real prices and stock. Seven thousand five hundred to set up.

> **[AUTOMATE · F-commerce]** Selling on Facebook? The F-commerce Kit adds an order sheet, a fake-order check and a brand refresh — about fifteen percent less than buying each part.

> **[AUTOMATE · Lead]** Real estate or coaching? The Lead Machine asks the right questions, books visits on WhatsApp and follows up on day one, three and seven.

> **[GROW · Ads]** Step four: grow. Ads that start WhatsApp conversations, not just likes — with a weekly report. Twelve thousand a month after setup; your ad budget goes straight to Meta.

> **[Which one]** So, which one is yours? Selling on Facebook — the F-commerce Kit. Real estate or coaching — the Lead Machine. Clinic, salon or restaurant — Booking. Brand new — the Launch System. Not sure? Start with a SEE Audit.
