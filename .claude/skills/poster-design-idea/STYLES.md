# Style Catalog

There are 9 styles, built from 27 reference posters in `references/`. The same
groups are on the Figma page "🖼 Idea Collection".

Reference posters are for inspiration only. Never copy their brand names,
logos, mascots or exact layouts. Take the idea behind the layout, not the
artwork.

## S01 · Bold Headline Hero & Offer
- **Layout:** Giant 1–2 word headline at the top (sometimes outlined or with
  mixed weights). The product hero sits in the center, often on a wooden table
  or bowl. CTA pill or price badge at the bottom. Logo top-left.
- **Color:** Warm yellow→orange or deep red radial gradient. Faint pattern
  (blobs, food line icons) behind.
- **Type:** Heavy condensed sans for the headline, script for the accent word,
  small sans for contact info.
- **Extras:** Starburst price badge ("Only 15$"), QR code, checker band,
  "Special Offer" tag.
- **Purpose:** Stop the scroll and state the offer in under one second.
- **Assets:** B01, B02, B09, F*, PR01, I04 (splash behind the product).

## S02 · Human Moment
- **Layout:** A person (chef, hands) holds the product toward the camera.
  Headline in script on the left, location or tagline on the right.
- **Color:** Maroon or brand red, warm light.
- **Purpose:** Shows that real people make it, so it reads as trustworthy,
  fresh and local.
- **Assets:** P01, P02 (pending), B02.

## S03 · Concept Storytelling (surreal series)
- **Layout:** Small two-weight headline at top-left ("Burger *O'Clock*"). One
  big visual metaphor fills the rest: mirror reflection, monster eating, clock
  made of ingredients, giant guy in an armchair, food critic. Logo top-right,
  tiny footer text.
- **Color:** Bright red→orange gradient, the same across the whole series.
- **Purpose:** People remember an idea more than a price. It suits campaign
  series and gets shared.
- **Assets:** B09, P04, PR05, I01 (pending), I02, C02 (pending).

## S04 · Dark Smoky Fire Offer
- **Layout:** Dark charcoal background with smoke, embers and fire. Food on a
  slate or wooden board. Script plus bold headline. Round "% OFF" badge,
  "Free Delivery" in script, pill "ORDER NOW".
- **Color:** Black/graphite with orange or yellow accents.
- **Purpose:** Looks premium and hot or spicy, and still sells a deal.
- **Assets:** B03, E01, PR03, I05, F09.

## S05 · Split Typography & Sunburst Special
- **Layout A:** One huge word ("PIZZA PIZZA"), split by the food in the
  middle. Slices bleed in from the corners. Small stamp badge.
- **Layout B:** Yellow sunburst rays with a red wave at the bottom, two
  products, a round % badge and an ORDER NOW pill.
- **Purpose:** Loud and readable even as a tiny thumbnail.
- **Assets:** B02, B04 (pending), F03 (pending).

## S06 · Brand System (Checker Diner)
- **Layout:** Two colors only (royal blue + cream). Checker band, condensed
  display type, line-art burger mascot, QR "order on app". Lifestyle bite
  photos.
- **Purpose:** A consistent multi-post campaign, so the brand is recognizable
  at a glance.
- **Assets:** C01, B05 (pending), P03 (pending), F07.

## S07 · Hand-drawn Doodle Bakery
- **Layout:** Script tagline at the top. Huge chalky-textured product word
  with a brush underline. Product hero on a color blob. Arrow callouts ("Rich
  Chocolate Chips"), a brush-stroke badge ("Freshly Baked"), heart and "Perfect
  for every mood". Optional row of 3 features.
- **Color:** One bold background (blue, brown, yellow/cream) plus one accent.
- **Purpose:** Warm, homemade and friendly. Good for desserts and cafés.
- **Assets:** F11, F13, F16, I03a/b, G01, B07/B08 (pending).

## S08 · Paper-cut Illustrated
- **Layout:** Repeated bold words, a cartoon character holding the product,
  cut-paper texture, neon green + pink + yellow.
- **Purpose:** Playful street-brand mood that stands out in a feed full of
  photos.
- **Assets:** C03 (pending).

## S09 · Premium Tech Product
- **Layout:** Dark or cool gradient (graphite, navy-purple, ocean blue).
  Product tilted 30–45°, often in a hand or on a pedestal. Thin geometric sans
  headline, sometimes two sizes ("TRUE *Wire-less*"). Tag row at the top
  ("PRECISION • PERFORMANCE • DESIGN"). 2–4 feature callouts with small icons or
  arrows. Faded brand watermark. One neon accent (green) for the CTA.
- **Purpose:** Signals precision, quality and premium value.
- **Assets:** B12, B10/B11/B13 (pending), T01, T03, T04a/b, T06.

## Lessons learned (from real feedback)

### 2026-10-07: SKC Day 1, Joy's critique
1. **Who and what at a glance.** Every poster needs a visible brand bar:
   logo, name, and a category word such as "রেস্টুরেন্ট". Never only a tiny
   corner logo.
2. **Category cues.** A restaurant must *read* as a restaurant without
   words: menu-card frames, food typography (script plus bold), utensil or
   skewer motifs, and food on a real table.
3. **An explicit CTA.** It answers "what do I do now?" as a verb plus an
   object, for example "স্লিপ দেখান, বসে পড়ুন". Add a phone number or
   Messenger, and the exact address.
4. **Use local objects, not generic UI icons.** A repair slip beats a
   battery icon. The metaphor must come from the user's real world, or it
   looks cheap.
5. **Answer who, why, how and where on every offer poster.**

### 2026-10-07: SKC Day 1, Joy's second note (C v1 → C v2)
6. **Food is the hero.** The main items are big, bold and close to the camera
   (they can bleed off the edge). The background is the real place, pushed
   back with blur and warmth, but properly arranged.
7. **Minimal text.** Use only: brand (small), one headline, a price seal, and
   one line of action + where. Move extra explanation (mechanics, Wi-Fi,
   payment) to the caption or the comments, not onto the poster.
