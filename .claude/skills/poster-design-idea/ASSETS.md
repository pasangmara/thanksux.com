# Asset Library

These are raw assets for building posters, stored in `assets/` and mirrored on
the Figma page "🧩 Asset Library". All of them were generated with ChatGPT
from the prompts in the "Pending" section, and none has text on it.

`bg` column:
- **black:** Remove the black or use Screen blend for effects. Dark items
  (chocolate, the slate board) are hard to cut out from black.
- **white:** Remove the white or use Multiply blend.
- **full:** A full-frame background.
