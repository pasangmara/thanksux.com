# Food Poster System (built from R28–R31, 2026-10-07)

These are Joy's references for a "high-performance" food poster: the product
is big, close and bold; the colour builds hunger; the type is custom; the
price is seen first. The source posters are in `references/` as R28–R31.

## 1. What each reference does best

| Ref | Strongest thing | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **R28 Biryani** (orange radial + white floor) | **Depth stage** | Orange glows behind the plate, and a soft grey floor with a contact shadow makes the plate *sit* in space. Steam and flying leaves add motion and freshness. |
| **R29 Shawarma** (cream + red swoosh) | **Info chip row** | A red band ties the two wraps together and pushes them forward. A faded giant word repeats in the background as texture. Price, delivery time and location sit in 3 equal chips, so they're read in one sweep. |
| **R30 Taste & Go** (giant type + hands) | **Type-as-background + hands** | Huge red condensed letters fill the frame and the products overlap them: instant depth, and the brand is felt without reading. Hands make it human, real and "for me". Thin leader lines name each item. A black price disc sits right between the products, where the eye already is. |
| **R31 Paket Combo** (red + splash + crossed price) | **Price drama + reflection** | The old price is crossed and the new price is big and bright: the deal is understood in 0.5s. A liquid splash gives freshness and motion. A reflection on a white floor makes it look premium and appetising. A script word + grunge condensed word make the headline. |

## 2. Hierarchy that works (reading order in under 3 sec)
1. **Food hero**: 45–60% of the frame, close, slightly overlapping the edges
   or the type.
2. **Headline**: 1–3 words, condensed heavy, 12–20% of the height.
3. **Price**: a high-contrast disc or tag, placed *touching* the hero,
   at the eye's first landing point (between the items, or upper-right of the
   hero). Never isolated in a corner.
4. **CTA**: a solid pill with an arrow, in the lower third. A different
   colour from the price.
5. **Info row**: location, phone, time. Small, aligned, one line or chips.
6. **Brand**: small, top centre or top corner.

## 3. Placement, balance, depth: the toolkit
- **Three depth layers:** background (colour/glow/giant type) → hero
  (sharp, with shadow) → foreground accents (leaves, splash, steam, a hand
  entering from the edge, sometimes slightly blurred).
- **Overlap:** the hero covers part of the giant type (R30) or the band
  (R29). Overlap = depth, for free.
- **Ground it:** a contact shadow + a floor line or reflection (R28, R31).
  Food that floats with no shadow looks fake.
- **Diagonal balance:** big item lower-left, secondary item upper-right
  (R30), or one hero centred with symmetrical side accents (R28).
- **Edge bleed:** let items cross the frame edge; it feels bigger and closer.
- **Motion:** steam, splash, a flying leaf or slice: "caught mid-moment".

## 4. Colour system that builds hunger
- **Warm hues:** red `#C8102E`, tomato `#E2401C`, saffron/orange `#F57C00`,
  turmeric yellow `#F5B400`. Warm colours read as heat, spice and energy.
- **Neutral stage:** cream `#F6EBDD` or white floor. It makes the food the
  most saturated thing in the frame.
- **Dark anchor:** espresso brown `#3B1F14` or black for headline/price. It
  gives contrast without cooling the mood.
- **Fresh accent:** green (leaf/herb) in small doses only. Freshness, and a
  complementary pop against red.
- **Avoid:** cold blue/grey backgrounds and desaturated food (R12-style dark
  is fine for "smoky/premium", not for value deals).
- **SKC mapping:** logo brown + ochre are the base. Add red/orange as the
  appetite stage and cream as the floor.

## 5. Typography system (Bangla + English)
| Role | Bangla | English | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Giant headline | **Noto Sans Bengali Condensed Black** (wdth 62, wght 900) | **Anton** | All-caps feel, tight leading (0.9), 12–20% of height, can sit behind food |
| Accent word | **Galada** | **Yellowtail** | Script, rotated −4°, overlaps the headline corner |
| Labels / chips | Anek Bangla Bold | Anek Bangla Bold | 26–34px at 1080w |
| Body (rare) | Anek Bangla Medium | — | One line max |

In Figma, use the same families (Noto Sans Bengali has Condensed Black;
Anton, Galada and Anek Bangla are available).

## 6. Price-tag system
- **Disc:** black or red circle; "মাত্র" small on top, number huge (Anton or
  Condensed Black), ৳ smaller than the digits.
- **Crossed price:** use only when a *real* old price exists. Never invent a
  discount.
- **Placement:** touching the hero, on the eye path, rotated −6° to −10°
  for energy.

## 7. CTA button system
- A solid pill, at least 90px high at 1080w, with a 4–8px soft glow or drop
  shadow, verb + object + arrow ("স্লিপ দেখান ▶", "অর্ডার করুন ▶").
- A colour that contrasts with both the background and the price (for
  example, ochre pill on red, red pill on cream).
- Phone/Messenger in small type next to or under it.
