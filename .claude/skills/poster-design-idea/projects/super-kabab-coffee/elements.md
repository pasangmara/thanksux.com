# Elements: SKC

Statuses below are for **Design A/B/C: Day 1, Repair Break.**

## Design variables
- **Scene:**
  - Background: an SKC table at warm evening light, not a granite counter.
  - Phone-repair bench on the other half (split scene).
- **Product photography:** in-use (hand lifting the coffee cup, steam) +
  layered (kabab build, day 2).
- **Manipulation:** a battery-meter overlay with a 5% → 100% transition, and
  the repair token turned into a table number.
- **Why it builds trust:**
  - It shows the real seat and the real coffee where the user is standing
    right now.
  - The price and time chips remove doubt.
- **Color:** taken from the logo. Espresso brown `#3B1F14`, roast ochre
  `#E0A43A`, cream `#F6EBDD`, and a chili-red accent `#C8102E` for price
  only. Solid cream for the message side, a warm brown gradient for the
  scene side.
- **Motion idea:** the battery fills while the steam rises. A 3-second loop
  for Reels and Stories.
