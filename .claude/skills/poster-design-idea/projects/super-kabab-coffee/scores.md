## Joy's critique of A and B (what failed)
1. **The CTA was unclear.** There was no action ("what do I do now?").
2. **Who and what service were unclear.** The brand was tiny, so it could
   have been any café.
3. **There were no restaurant cues.** No menu feel and no food typography,
   so it didn't read as a restaurant at a glance.
4. **The 5% battery looked boring and cheap.** It is a generic UI icon, not
   a local object.
5. **Who gives it, why, how and where exactly** were all unanswered.

## How C v1 answers each point
- **Who:** a brand bar with the logo, "Super Kabab & Coffee" and "কাবাব ·
  কফি · রেস্টুরেন্ট | মোতালিব প্লাজা".
- **Why:** the script question "ফোন সারাতে দিয়েছেন?" plus the headline
  "অপেক্ষা নয়, টেবিল রেডি!".
- **How:** the **mobile repair slip** is the coupon. A red SKC stamp on it
  says "ওয়েটিং কম্বো ৳১৯৯". This object is local and real; the battery
  wasn't.
- **Restaurant cues:**
  - a menu-card offer (double border, corner ornaments, dotted price
    leader);
  - a kabab-skewer divider taken from the logo;
  - Galada script;
  - food on a real table.
- **CTA:** "স্লিপ দেখান, বসে পড়ুন ▶" plus "কল/মেসেজ: ০১৯৭২-৪৯৮৫৬১".
- **Where:** a corridor inset with "হেঁটে মাত্র ২ মিনিট", plus the full
  address strip.
- **Payment and extras:** বিকাশ · নগদ · ক্যাশ, and ফ্রি ওয়াইফাই.

## A/B test suggestion
- **Variant 1:** C with the slip coupon.
- **Variant 2:** C without the slip; plain "ওয়েটিং কম্বো ৳১৯৯".
- **Measure:** slips collected at the counter, and Messenger questions.
  Run each for 3 days as a boosted post to people within 1 km of
  Motalib Plaza.

## Chance estimate
About **60%** chance of beating the page's average photo post on comments
and shares.
- **Reasons:** a local insight, a clear mechanic and a price shown.
- **Risks:**
  - the food photos are stand-ins;
  - the owner hasn't approved the ৳199 combo yet;
  - the exact entrance or floor isn't shown.
