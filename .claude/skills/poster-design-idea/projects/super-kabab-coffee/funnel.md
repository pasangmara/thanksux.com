## 3 hooks (each meant to be understood at a glance, unexpected, and something people imagined but never saw)

1. **"ফোন রিচার্জ হচ্ছে, আপনিও হোন" (A2)**
   - **Visual:** split scene. Left: a phone on the repair bench at 5%
     battery. Right: a person at an SKC table with coffee and kabab, a
     "battery 100%" icon above them.
   - **2 ways to show it:** (a) a literal battery meter; (b) a repair token
     number turned into an SKC table number: "আপনার টোকেন ১৭? টেবিল ১৭ রেডি".
2. **"দোকান ছাড়তে হবে না" (A1)**
   - **Visual:** a lunch tray travelling through the plaza corridor to a
     shop counter, with a ৳ price tag and a "১০ মিনিট" timer.
   - **2 ways:** (a) a top-down map of the plaza with a route line; (b) a
     shop counter with phones on one side and a full plate on the other.
3. **"শাটার নামলো, আড্ডা শুরু" (A1 + A4)**
   - **Visual:** a shop shutter half down at 8pm, warm light spilling from
     SKC across the lane, and a platter for 4 with borhani and coffee.
   - **2 ways:** (a) a shutter-to-table transition (also the motion idea);
     (b) a table seen from above with 4 hands reaching in.

## Both-side win
- **User:** a full, tasty plate or coffee under ৳300, fast, with a seat or
  shop delivery and no bill shock.
- **Maker:** fill the seats in the afternoon and evening, raise the average
  ticket from about ৳220 to about ৳300 with add-ons (own borhani, firni,
  coffee), and get repeat visits.
- **Shared win:** a **"Plaza Pass" combo system** with fixed price, fixed
  time and your own drink. The user saves time and money; the maker earns
  margin on house drinks plus repeat visits.

## Combo / package proposals
Prices are proposals. The owner must check them against cost.

| Combo | Contents | Proposed price | Audience | Gap it fills | Sell chance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lunch Express** | Tehari or roast polao + borhani, served in 10 min or delivered to the shop inside the plaza | ৳249 | A1 | No time, needs a fixed price | High |
| **Repair Break** | Coffee + firni (or soup) + free Wi-Fi (a charging strip is suggested to the owner) | ৳199 | A2 | Bored waiting, no seat | Medium–high (unique) |
| **Adda Platter for 4** | Mini kabab platter + naan + 4 borhani or coffee | ৳999 | A1, A4 | Evening hangout value | Medium–high |
| **Team Lunch Box (5+)** | Set menu + drink, pre-order by message | ৳229 per person | A3 | Office group ordering | Medium |
| **Plaza Card** | Buy 10 lunches, 11th free (stamp card) | n/a | A1 | Repeat visits | High for the core |

## Campaign definition
- **Goal:** make Motalib Plaza people and nearby groups see SKC as "the
  plaza's own place to eat, wait and hang out", and bring them in.
- **Length:** **14 days.**
  - Days 1–7: connection and story.
  - Days 8–14: test the packages and push sales.
  - Why 14: the core audience is local and repeat. One week builds
    recognition; the second week shows which combo sells.
- **Needed:**
  - a photo or video shoot (the shot list is in `elements.md`);
  - a table tent with a QR code to the review page;
  - stamp cards;
  - a code word;
  - a person replying to comments within 1 hour (already: "Replies within
    an hour").
- **Start point:** about 1–7 reactions per photo post and 0 comments. The
  best-ever post had 340 reactions.
- **Finish goal:**
  - ≥30 average reactions and ≥5 comments per post;
  - ≥50 code-word redemptions;
  - ≥20 shop-delivery orders inside the plaza;
  - Google reviews up from 7 to ≥30.
- **Check day: day 10.**
  - (a) Connection: comments, shares, messages.
  - (b) Product insight: which combo wins, so that one gets developed (for
    example, making Lunch Express permanent).

## User journey map
| Step | Sees | Does | Feels / asks | Make it beautiful |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| See | A post in the feed | Scrolls | "এটা কি আমার আশেপাশে?" | Plaza landmark plus a familiar situation in the visual |
| Stop | The hook visual | Pauses 2 sec | "আরে, এটা তো আমি!" | One idea per poster, a big custom headline |
| Understand | Combo + price + time | Reads 1 line | "কত? কতক্ষণ?" | A price chip and a time chip, always in the same spot |
| Want | Steam, a full plate, a table | Taps "see more" | "ভালো দেখাচ্ছে" | Real photos with motion (steam, pour) |
| Ask | — | Comments or messages | "দোকানে দিবেন?" | A pinned reply template; answer in under 1 hour |
| Buy | Code word, menu | Walks in or orders | "সঠিক দাম তো?" | Same price everywhere, VAT shown |
| Receive / open | A box or tray | Eats | "পরিমাণ ঠিক আছে?" | Branded tray liner with a "Thanks, plaza family" note and the QR card |
| Use again | Stamp card | Comes back | "আবার আসবো" | Stamp card plus "your vote decides the next item" |
