# Research: SKC (time box 30 min, done 2026-10-07)

## Key questions → answers

1. **Who is within 1 km?**
   - Motalib Plaza is a mobile accessories, wholesale and repair hub at 8
     Paribag, Hatirpool [FE, Hatirpool wiki]. Confidence: **high**.
   - Repair customers wait for display or battery jobs. Confidence:
     **high**; SKC's own Aug 30 post says the same.
   - Offices around Bangla Motor and Kawran Bazar, and students in the
     Shahbag, DU and Elephant Road belt. Confidence: **medium** (location
     inference).
2. **Budget and behaviour:**
   - Traders and staff can't leave the shop for long. Lunch is rushed, and
     evening after closing is free time.
   - Repair waiters have 30–90 minutes to kill (assumption, medium).
   - Spend is under ৳300 (Joy).
3. **Competitors:**
   - **Star Kabab** has branches at Kawran Bazar and New Elephant Road. It is
     a cheap full-plate kabab meal and a known name (Star Kabab wiki; older
     plate prices ৳140–180, likely higher now).
   - Local hotels: cheapest and fastest, with no ambience.
   - Coffee shops: ambience and Wi-Fi, but no full meals.
   - **SKC's possible gap: the only place that does both a real meal and
     proper coffee under one roof, inside the mobile market.**
4. **Crowd voice** (synthesis of public reviews of Dhaka kacchi and kabab
   places, not a survey):

   | Theme | Majority voice | Confidence |
   |---|---|---|
   | Waiting | "Ordered, said 15 min, served in 40" | high |
   | Portion | "Piece too small for the price" | high |
   | Quality | "Kacchi too oily", "mutton dry"; praise for "less oily, juicy" | high |
   | Service | "Can't get a waiter", "staff rude", "booking not honoured" | medium |
   | Bill shock | "Some items surprisingly priced" | medium |
   | Payment | "No online payment" is a complaint | medium |
   | Social (Bangladesh) | Food posts are among the most common on Bangladeshi Facebook. People reply to offers that give real value or a free item. | medium |

   **What this means for SKC:** promise **speed, clear price, a full portion
   and less oil.** Those are the category's pain points.
5. **Combos that work in Dhaka:** a value combo with your own drink
   (borhani instead of a bottled soft drink), a family or friends pack, a
   free add-on on a code word, and loyalty cards (assumption, medium).
6. **SKC's real difference:**
   - Starbucks beans, with proof.
   - A trial kitchen that tests before launching ("খুঁতখুঁতে"), and
     "your feedback decides" (borhani, brisket).
   - Meal + coffee under one roof.
   - The kitchen specialty is still unknown.
7. **Topic of the moment (Oct 2026):** the end of the rain season (rain plus
   khichuri still works), the evening adda season starting, and a
   new-item curiosity (brisket, borhani).
8. **Delivery:** both channels. Delivery inside the plaza to shops is
   unknown and is the biggest opportunity.
