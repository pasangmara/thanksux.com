# Problem Hunt, Pre-mortem & Owner Message: SKC

## Real problems today
1. **Photos:**
   - Granite counter, flat light, blurry bottle.
   - The food looks smaller and cheaper than it is.
   - The ৳260–320 price then feels "high for what I see" (Joy's own
     reaction).
2. **Identity split:**
   - "Kabab & Coffee" plus an Indian menu plus Chinese plus combos means
     nobody knows what SKC is *for*.
   - Proposed line: **"দিনে পেট ভরা, সন্ধ্যায় মন ভরা"**: a meal in the day,
     adda in the evening.
3. **Choice overload:**
   - The menu posters show 9–12 prices at once.
   - The best post worked *despite* this, because price was visible.
4. **Unanswered price:** the brisket post's only comment was "price".
5. **Borrowed story:**
   - Starbucks® is used as the hero.
   - Even with proof, avoid their logo or ® styling in ads.
   - Say "made with Starbucks beans" as a supporting line, and make SKC's
     own ritual the hero.
6. **Invisible trust:** 100% recommend, but only 7 reviews. No faces, no
   kitchen, no cleanliness shown.

## Pre-mortem: "the campaign failed because SKC didn't feel worth it"
| Failure reason | Design / product fix |
|---|---|
| Food looked cheap in photos | A one-time shoot: warm table scene, steam, hands, 45° and top-down angles (see `elements.md`) |
| People didn't know the price or VAT | A fixed price chip on every post: "ভ্যাটসহ ৳249" |
| Lunch took too long | Lunch Express promises 10 min; the kitchen pre-preps tehari and polao for 1–3pm |
| Plaza staff couldn't leave the shop | Shop delivery inside the plaza by a runner, ordered on WhatsApp or Messenger |
| Felt like "just another hotel" | Own the plaza: repair-wait lounge, Plaza Card, plaza-family language |
| Comments ignored | Reply template + pinned comment with prices in under 1 hour |

## Zero-confusion check (order → delivery → open → use)
| Question | Where it gets answered |
|---|---|
| Where exactly in the plaza? | Every poster footer: "মোতালিব প্লাজা গলি, ৮ পরিবাগ" + a mini map icon; a pinned post with an entrance photo |
| Is there a seat? | Repair Break and Adda posts show the seats; a "২০–৫০ জন বসার জায়গা" line |
| How long? | A time chip (10 min express) |
| Price with VAT? | A price chip "ভ্যাটসহ" |
| Delivery to my shop? | "প্লাজার ভেতরে দোকানে পৌঁছে দেই": confirm with the owner first |
| Payment? | bKash / Nagad / cash icons: confirm with the owner |
| Charging or Wi-Fi? | Repair Break poster: confirm with the owner |

## Message to the owner (plain words)
1. **Keep your best idea and make it daily.** The price menu got 340 likes.
   Your customers decide on price first, so show price clearly, but for 1–3
   items per post, not 12.
2. **Use your own borhani in combos** instead of bottled soft drinks. It is
   your product, likely a better margin, and gives a reason to come.
3. **Start shop delivery inside Motalib Plaza.** Your biggest crowd can't
   leave their shops.
4. **Become the plaza's waiting lounge.** Add charging points and say so.
   No competitor can copy your location.
5. **Do one proper photo or video shoot.** The food is good, but the photos
   undersell it.
6. **Answer "price" comments within an hour, and pin the price.**
7. **Ask every happy table for a Google review** (QR table tent). Seven
   reviews isn't enough trust.
