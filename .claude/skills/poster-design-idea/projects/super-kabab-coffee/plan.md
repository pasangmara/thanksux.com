# Plan Card: Super Kabab & Coffee (SKC)

- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Client:** Super Kabab & Coffee, Motalib Plaza, Dhaka. Facebook page has
  7.5K followers, 656 posts and is verified. Price level shown as $$.
- **Category:** Restaurant and café (kabab, Indian, rice dishes, coffee,
  drinks).
- **Complexity:** Complex. There are many items and several audiences, and
  the job needs an offer or package system, not just one product.

## Starting audit (from Joy's screenshots, before the interview)

| Seen | What it means |
|---|---|
| Posts are a plain photo plus a long caption, with 1–7 reactions, 0 comments and 1–2 shares each | Weak scroll-stop. Nothing invites a comment or save. |
| Photos are on a granite counter or under flat light. The borhani bottle is blurry. | Food looks cheaper than its price. There is no table or ambience story. |
| Captions are generic ("আজই চলে আসুন") and repeat the location | There is no reason to choose SKC over the café next door. |
| The coffee is claimed to use "Starbucks Coffee Bean" and tagged #StarbucksCoffee | Possible trust or trademark risk. It also borrows another brand's story instead of building SKC's own. **To verify.** |
| Khichuri on a rainy day (topic of the moment) and borhani "test basis, your feedback decides" | Good instincts. These are the hooks to build on. |
| The cover is crowded and the logo is a detailed illustration | Needs a simpler brand system (2–3 colors, one display font). |
| 100% recommend, but only 7 reviews | Trust exists but isn't visible. Reviews and UGC need a push. |

## Research plan (≤8 questions, 3–4 competitors)

Questions:
1. Who works or studies within 1 km of Motalib Plaza, and when do they eat?
   Groups to check: the mobile market traders, offices around Karwan Bazar
   and Bangla Motor, students, hospital visitors (assumption).
2. For each group: budget per meal, meal time, group size, and what they
   value (speed, quantity, AC seating, adda space).
3. Which nearby kabab, kacchi and café places get engagement, and how? Look
   at their hook, photos, offers and comments.
4. What do Dhaka diners praise and complain about at kabab and coffee places
   (crowd voice: reviews, comments, food groups)?
5. Which combos and packages work in Dhaka (student deal, office lunch box,
   adda platter, couple set, meeting package)?
6. What does SKC actually do that's different (charcoal, marinade, coffee
   beans, space)? Mostly from your answers.
7. What is the topic of the moment (season, rain, events, office time,
   exams)?
8. Delivery: which apps, how fast, packaging quality?

Sources: Facebook pages and comments of competitors, Google Maps reviews, food
groups, foodpanda listings, and news or food features. **Results:** 4
competitors and ~10 crowd-voice points per audience. Each crowd-voice point is
labelled as synthesis, with a confidence level.
