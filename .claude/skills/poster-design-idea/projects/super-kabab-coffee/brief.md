## How much the answers helped: **≈60%**
- **Most useful:**
  - The core audience is shopkeepers and staff.
  - The spend is under ৳300.
  - The peak is afternoon and evening adda.
  - Combos worked before.
  - Together these fix the price band and the hook direction.
- **Option fit check:** "Awareness + footfall" fits. But under ৳300 with 20–50
  seats means the money is in **turnover and add-ons**, not premium tickets.
  The goal should become: footfall plus the **add-on rate** (borhani, firni,
  coffee).
- **Still unknown (needed to understand the full picture):**
  1. The best-selling item and its cost or margin.
  2. The kitchen specialty (charcoal? marinade?).
  3. Lunch-time crowd vs evening.
  4. Can they deliver to shops inside the plaza?
  5. Wi-Fi and phone charging at the tables?
  6. Payment options (bKash, Nagad, card).
  7. Opening hours.

## ⚠ Rule vs data tension
Rule 16 says to reveal the price late. But this page's own data shows the
price-menu post did **20–50× better** than anything else, because the core
audience decides on price first. **Proposal:** keep the funnel, but show a
*value anchor* ("৩০০ টাকার নিচে পুরো প্লেট") from day 1 and put the full price
ladder on day 5. **Joy decides.**
